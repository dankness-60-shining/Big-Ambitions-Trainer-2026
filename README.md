## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is an indie business and life simulation game developed by Hovgaard Games. It entered Early Access on March 10, 2023, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. Set in New York City, it combines role-playing, business management, economic simulation, and open-world life-sim mechanics.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides optional controls for money, personal needs, time, inventory, transportation, and business management. It is designed around Big Ambitions' systems, including property ownership, store operations, employee management, logistics, investments, and personal well-being.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with business layouts, test logistics networks, build large property portfolios, reduce repetitive management tasks, or explore Big Ambitions without normal financial and time limitations.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement option to make travel around New York more convenient. Time controls can reduce downtime during commutes, shopping trips, property visits, and deliveries. Vehicle-related assistance is useful when moving between businesses or transporting goods.

### Tools & Inventory

Big Ambitions relies on physical inventory management. Products must be purchased, transported, stored, and supplied to businesses. Inventory assistance helps reduce repeated supply runs and makes it easier to test store layouts, product selections, and warehouse distribution.

### World & Environment

The game takes place in an open city where the character physically travels between apartments, businesses, suppliers, offices, warehouses, and other locations. Financial resources can be used to acquire property, furnish spaces, buy vehicles, expand operations, and invest in real estate or funds.

### Combat & Weapons

Big Ambitions has no conventional combat system. The trainer therefore focuses on business, economy, travel, inventory, time, and personal-needs systems rather than weapons, combat statistics, or enemy encounters.
